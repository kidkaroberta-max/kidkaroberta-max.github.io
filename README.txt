ROBERTA BUSINESS HUB WEBSITE

1. Open index.html in a browser to preview.
2. BEFORE PUBLISHING: open index.html in a text editor, find WHATSAPP_NUMBER and insert your business WhatsApp number in international format (e.g. 231XXXXXXXXX).
3. Upload ALL files and the images folder to GitHub Pages or another static hosting provider.
4. Prices and stock can be edited in the products list inside index.html. products.json is a reference copy, not automatically connected.
5. This is a static website. Stock is DISPLAY stock and does NOT automatically decrease for other customers or after WhatsApp orders. Update it manually after sales. Cart persists on the customer device.
6. Checkout prepares a WhatsApp message, not a verified payment or automated order database. Confirm payment before fulfillment.
7. Confirm all delivery fees and product specifications before publishing.
